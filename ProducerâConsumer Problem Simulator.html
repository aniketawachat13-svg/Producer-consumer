How Producer–Consumer Works

Producer: The Car Factory produces cars and places them into the shared parking buffer.

Buffer: The Parking Area is a limited shared resource where produced cars wait before being consumed.

Consumer: The Customer/Dealer removes cars from the parking buffer.

Semaphore: Semaphores control the number of empty and occupied parking slots and prevent simultaneous unsafe access.

Producer: wait(empty) → wait(mutex) → add car → signal(mutex) → signal(full)

Consumer: wait(full) → wait(mutex) → remove car → signal(mutex) → signal(empty)
